ABOUT KAMIWA

A small studio in Kyoto that plans one journey at a time.

Kamiwa means paper and harmony. We began in 2014 as three guides who kept meeting the same tired travellers: people who had seen everything and remembered very little. We started planning trips for friends, then friends of friends.

Today we shape around forty private journeys a year. Never more. Each one is built by the guide who will meet you at the station, so the plan and the person are the same thing.
